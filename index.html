Meet Kissu

Hi, I’m Kissu, the creative eye and storyteller behind Shot by Kissu.

My creative journey began in 2020, rooted in a love for visual storytelling, meaningful details, and the beauty found in everyday moments. With a background in law and social media management, I bring a unique blend of creativity, intention, and attention to detail to everything I do.

In 2026, my passion for storytelling led me into the world of weddings and love stories, where I discovered a new kind of magic.

There’s something incredibly special about witnessing love in its most genuine form. The anticipation before a proposal, the excitement of an engagement, the intimacy of an elopement, or the overwhelming joy of a wedding day.

For me, it’s never just about creating beautiful content. It’s about preserving moments and bringing stories to life, thoughtfully and authentically.

Whether it’s a love story, a brand’s vision, or a milestone worth celebrating, I’d be honored to tell a little piece of your story.

Questions

How far in advance should I book?

For weddings, 6–12 months ahead is ideal. For brand shoots and social media projects, 2–4 weeks is usually enough. Events depend on scale, so reach out early to lock in your date.

What does pricing look like?

Every project is different! Fill out the form and I’ll send available packages or a custom quote where applicable.

How long until I receive my final edit?

All edited videos are delivered within 48 hours after your event.

Do you travel outside Atlanta?

Absolutely. I travel, and travel fees are quoted separately depending on location and date.

What equipment do you use?

All content is captured on my iPhone 17 Pro Max.

Can I request specific songs for my video?

Yes, with a caveat. I use licensed music libraries to stay copyright-compliant on socials. If you have a specific track in mind, I’ll find the closest licensed match or work with you on alternatives.

Do you offer raw footage?

Yes, all raw footage is delivered 24 hours after your event.

What happens if the weather is bad on shoot day?

Rain makes films gorgeous, honestly. I come prepared with weather covers, backup lighting, and a rain-or-shine attitude.

What happens if you’re unable to attend my event?

In the unlikely event that I’m unable to attend, you’ll be notified promptly, and I’ll make every effort to arrange a trusted replacement. If a replacement cannot be secured, a full refund will be issued.
